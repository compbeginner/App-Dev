# 🗡️ Demon Slayer (Kimetsu no Yaiba)

## **Overview**
*Demon Slayer* is a Japanese anime series based on the manga by Koyoharu Gotouge.  
It follows **Tanjiro Kamado**, a kind-hearted boy who becomes a demon slayer after his family is slaughtered and his sister Nezuko is turned into a demon.

---

## **Main Characters**
- **Tanjiro Kamado**: The protagonist, known for his compassion and determination.  
- **Nezuko Kamado**: Tanjiro’s younger sister, transformed into a demon but retains her humanity.  
- **Zenitsu Agatsuma**: A cowardly yet powerful swordsman when unconscious.  
- **Inosuke Hashibira**: A wild fighter raised by boars, wearing a boar’s head.  
- **Kyojuro Rengoku**: Flame Hashira, known for his fiery spirit and bravery.  

---

## **Key Themes**
1. **Family Bonds** – Tanjiro’s devotion to Nezuko drives the story.  
2. **Perseverance** – Characters endure immense struggles to protect humanity.  
3. **Good vs Evil** – The eternal battle between demon slayers and demons.
